Fellowship details, run on 2026-11-02 (three live runs, one into a scratch copy, one into the dev store, one into the demo store, all with the same counts). Of 76 records: 64 pages fetched, 2 skipped by robots.txt (`www.cdc.gov`, `www2.ed.gov`), 10 errors (7 HTTP 404 from dead links in the SDFA list, 3 HTTP 403 from `www.aauw.org`, `www.noaa.gov`, `www.peacecorps.gov`, which refuse automated requests and were not worked around), 4 deadlines, 11 amounts (hand-checked against the saved page text; one more, a Payne fellowship figure, was dropped by the rule below), 2 ambiguous (several different amounts on one page, none kept), 0 non-USD skipped. Records without a value keep "not stated". Most SDFA pages give campus deadlines without a year ("June 1"), so they yield no deadline: a year is never inferred.

Parsing rules (all in code, no model reads the page):
- **Text:** `<main>` or `<article>` when present, else the page; script, style, nav, footer, forms removed; split into lines and sentences.
- **Deadline:** a sentence with a deadline word (deadline, due, applications close, submit by, apply by, closes on, no later than) and a full date with a year, on or after the fetch date. A date after "opens", "notified", "announced" and similar words is not taken. A sentence about faculty or staff is skipped, because the list is for students. A campus or Temple deadline beats a sponsor one; then the earliest date. When the sentence says "for these regions", the sentence before it is kept in the quote.
- **Amount:** a sentence with a dollar amount and an award word (award, stipend, scholarship, fellowship, grant, funding, prize, up to, worth). "up to $X" is a ceiling, "$X to $Y" and "between $X and $Y" a range, a single "$X" both ends. Sentences with million, billion, fee, cost, charge, tuition, salary, or a per-month, per-year or similar rate are skipped, and so is $0. Euro, pound and other currencies are skipped and counted. Different amounts on one page mean none is kept.
- **Ended awards:** a page that says the award was terminated, discontinued, suspended or is no longer accepting applications keeps no value.
- **Quote:** the exact sentence, trimmed to about 300 characters around the match.

### Temple dataset rules in the v1 briefs (S49 and S50)

These rules read the stored Temple records. They change what a brief shows, not what the pages say. Every date shown is the page's own, copied by code.

- **Past-cycle dates:** a Temple award whose page date is more than 31 days old is not ruled out. It gets "may be eligible" and the code-written line "The page shows a past date, <date>. Check the page for this year's date." Most of these awards recur each year. The code is `PAST_CYCLE_DAYS` in `perch/student.py` and `perch/eligibility.py`.
- **Very old page dates:** for Temple internal funding, a page date more than 396 days old is not treated as last year's cycle. The page is left out, with the reason "page last dated <date>". The code is `PAST_CYCLE_MAX_DAYS` in `perch/eligibility.py`.
- **Temple internal funding:** the Temple pages that offer faculty funding reach researcher briefs through their own rules, with a Temple applicant assumed. They show in a separate "Temple internal" group and are never mixed with federal leads. A page named for an NIH mechanism or for students leaves that group.
- **Open-anytime calls:** a posted federal call with no close date stays in a brief only when its own note says proposals are accepted at any time, or that it stays open until superseded, and its posted date is within ten years. The card shows the record's own words as the deadline.
- **Placeholder close dates:** a close date more than ten years out is never shown as a date. The card shows the record's own note instead.
- **Notices:** a title that names a notice of intent, a request for information or a sources-sought notice is left out in its own hidden group, because it funds nothing.
- **Student level:** when a Temple page tags an award "mixed" or gives no tag, the award's own name sets the level. A postdoctoral fellowship leaves the student pool. An F31 or predoctoral fellowship filed under faculty is treated as graduate.
- **Staff awards:** five awards that Temple pages file with the student scholarships are for staff and are left out of student briefs. They were chosen by hand on 4 Nov 2026 from the stored records. Awards tagged faculty or postdoc are left out as well.
- **Duplicates:** copies of one award are merged when their names match after plurals and the words program, scholarship, scholar and award are dropped. This works across Temple pages and the fellowships office, and within one source. The fellowships-office copy is kept, with its level and class year. Two names stay apart: a degree-level scholarship and Temple's need-based grant. An amount stated only on a dropped copy is not carried over yet.
