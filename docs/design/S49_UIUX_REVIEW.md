# S49 UI/UX review (fresh context, 2026-11-04)

This review covers about 30 screenshots from build/s48_walk_light and build/s48_walk_dark, at 1280 and 360 wide, and the owner's notes. Each item is traced to the code. File paths are under web/ unless they say otherwise. Items already queued in S48_UI_REVIEW.md or S48_UI_DESIGN.md are left out. Researchers are not named; "the researcher" means the walk's researcher brief.

## Must-fix

- **U1 School chips show the wrong counts:** a search for "zzzzqqxx" gives "0 matches", but the chips still read "CST 25" and "Fox School of Business 22". The counts come from the unfiltered list. Either recount them for the current search and level, or drop the numbers while a search is active. browse.js:206-212, w1280-awards-empty.
- **U2 Compare titles start with the record number:** each row in the Compare table reads "26-519: MPS Chemistry…". The owner flagged this on the cards. The prefix is added on the server. Show the title alone, and put the number on a second muted line or in Evidence. perch/compare.py:122, w1280-brief-researcher-compare, w360-handoff-ready.
- **U3 One warning repeated on every student card:** each student card shows the same amber box, "The explanation was withheld because it failed the citation checks twice." Five of these boxes in a row make the page look broken, and they are louder than the titles. Say it once above the list, using the existing `.common-lines` box. On each card, keep only a quiet one-line hint. student.js:346, app.js:1739, styles.css:1288, w1280-brief-student.
- **U4 The page scrollbar is invisible:** `scrollbar-color: transparent` hides the page scrollbar. It only shows when the mouse is within 8 px of the right edge. Keyboard and touchpad users lose the cue for where they are on long lists. Remove the rule and the mousemove toggle. styles.css:79, app.js:2287-2288.
- **U5 The disabled office hint is unreadable:** `.office-opt.off` sets opacity .6 on the whole row. That fades the hint "no kept industry leads" to about 2.5:1 contrast. That hint is the reason the option is off. Fade only the radio and the label, and keep the hint at full `--ink-3`. styles.css:845, w1280-handoff-busy.
- **U6 The daily-allowance error repeats per card:** after the allowance is used, each card shows a red error. The allowance applies to the whole account. Show it once as a page-level info notice. The notice should say when the allowance comes back, if the server gives that time. cover.js:136-145, w1280-cover-error.

## Should-fix

- **U7 Four different left edges:** the header text starts at about 52 px, the guest banner at 66, list pages at 100, and the footer at 98. Give the banner, the footer and the main column the same container as `.bar-in` (1212 px wide with 16 px padding). styles.css:108, 967, 1253, w1280-signin-unavailable, w1280-my-perch.
- **U8 Step count said twice:** the finished progress card reads "Built in 7 s · 8 of 8 steps" and then "Steps (8 of 8 done)". When the brief is done, drop the count from the Steps summary. app.js:880, app.js:922, w1280-brief-researcher.
- **U9 Disabled buttons show a busy cursor:** `.btn[disabled]` uses `cursor: progress`. Disabled buttons such as Continue and Compare look like they are loading. Use `not-allowed`, and keep `progress` for `aria-busy` only. styles.css:117, w1280-welcome.
- **U10 Sort sits in a different place on each side:** on the researcher side, Sort is on the same row as Grants and Industry, on the right. On the student side, the wider switch pushes Sort onto its own row on the left. Put Sort on the count row, the line that reads "10 options…", on both sides. app.js:1449, styles.css:1203, w1280-brief-student.
- **U11 Two names for one shortlist button:** the shortlist button says "Compare and hand off" on the researcher side and "Compare and draft a note" on the student side. Use one verb pattern on both, for example "Compare and draft". app.js:1051.
- **U12 Hand-off box too tall:** the email textarea has `rows: 18`. A five-line draft therefore sits in a 450 px empty box. Size the box to its content, with a minimum of 8 rows. handoff.js:130, w1280-handoff-draft.
- **U13 Draft again sits above the draft:** the "Draft again" button comes before the Subject and Email fields it replaces. Move it to the action row, next to Copy and Open in my mail app. handoff.js:114, w1280-handoff-draft.
- **U14 Points again sits above the points:** the "Points again" button comes before the points it replaces. Move it under the list. cover.js:130, w1280-cover-result, w360-cover-result.
- **U15 Cover heading repeats the title:** the cover heading reads "Points for your cover letter: NRSA Individual Predoctoral Fellowship (F31)". The card title is right above it. Shorten the heading to "Cover-letter points". cover.js pointsBlock, w1280-cover-result.
- **U16 Guest sign-in button on every card:** a guest sees "Sign in to get cover-letter points" on every card. Show it once above the list as a line with a link. On each card, keep a quiet text link at most. cover.js:15, cover.js:123, w1280-brief-researcher.
- **U17 Three sign-in links for a guest:** a guest has the Guest chip, a header "Sign in" and a banner "Sign in", all in view at once. Keep the banner link. Make the chip open a menu that includes Sign in. app.js:236, w1280-landing-guest.
- **U18 The guest banner cannot be closed:** the guest banner stays on every screen and takes 65 px. The sign-in-unavailable banner already has a close button. Give the guest banner one too, and remember the choice for the session. app.js:236, w1280-brief-student.
- **U19 Two guest paths on one screen:** the sign-in-unavailable landing shows both "Try a sample student" and "Try it as a guest". These two choices look the same to a visitor. Keep one. w1280-signin-unavailable.
- **U20 Empty white space in Scout:** the chat panel has a fixed window height. A short conversation therefore leaves about 150 px of blank space above the question box. Let the panel shrink to its content until it fills the window. styles.css:1149, scout.js:157, w1280-scout-clean.
- **U21 Two warnings for one Scout failure:** a flagged Scout answer shows an amber notice, then a model-text box that says the same thing again. Keep the notice. Drop the second sentence from the provenance line. scout.js:295, w1280-scout-flagged.
- **U22 Weak delete button in the dialog:** the final "Delete my data" button uses the soft brick fill with ink text. It looks no stronger than Cancel. Use a solid `--brick` fill with white text for the confirm button only. styles.css:1434, w360-delete-dialog.
- **U23 Search box shows two borders:** when the search field is focused, a blue ring is drawn inside the box's own border. That makes a box within a box. Move the ring to `.searchbox:focus-within`, and remove the inner outline. styles.css:949, w1280-awards-empty.
- **U24 Browse cards repeat "Not stated":** most Temple award cards show "Amount: Not stated" and "Deadline: Not stated". That is two rows of nothing on each card. When both are missing, show one muted line, "No amount or deadline on the page". The queued lead-card fix does not cover browse.js. browse.js:96-99, w1280-awards.
- **U25 A scope tag that reads like an error:** the tag "Scope not stated on the page" looks like a problem with the record. Drop it from the tag row, and let Evidence say it. browse.js:71, w1280-awards.
- **U26 Level filter has no visible label:** the level select has only a screen-reader label. On the funding page, "Any level" does not say what kind of level it means. Add a visible label, "Level of study". browse.js:239-242, w1280-funding.
- **U27 The phone Sort control is too wide:** under 560 px wide, the Sort control stretches to the full width, while the Grants and Industry switch does not. The two controls look unrelated. Size them the same way. styles.css:1201-1205, w360-brief-researcher.
- **U28 Rule-check pill fills its cell on phone:** in the phone Compare cards, the "May be eligible" pill stretches across its whole cell. Set `justify-self: start` on the pills in stacked cells. styles.css:1270, w360-brief-student-compare.
- **U29 Two heading styles in one card:** in "What steers the search", the heading "Your interests" is serif and the heading "Your achievements" is small sans. These are sibling headings, so give them one style. student.js:183, student.js:194, w1280-brief-student-profile.
- **U30 My Perch rows all look the same:** every student row is titled "Student brief". Each row also ends in the raw status word "done". Name each row by its date or its CV name. Show the status only when the brief is not finished. account.js:267-268, w1280-my-perch.
- **U31 Account and welcome pages lean left:** these forms are 680 px wide and sit on the left. That leaves the right 500 px empty at desktop width. Centre the column, or put the documents and delete cards in a second column. styles.css:1425-1430, w1280-account, w1280-welcome.
- **U32 Welcome heading too close to the header:** the welcome page has no Back link. Its h1 therefore sits about 18 px below the header, while other pages have about 80 px. Add the same top space. w1280-welcome.
- **U33 Sample banner too close to the heading:** on Sample records, the banner sits about 4 px under the h1. Use the page's normal 22 px gap. browse.js:305, w1280-samples.
- **U34 Unclear "To" line:** "To: left blank for you." reads oddly. Write "To: add the address yourself." student.js:491, handoff.js:120.
- **U35 Unclear account label:** the account label "Your sides" is unclear. Use "Using Perch as". account.js:214, w1280-account.
- **U36 Landing headline leaves one word alone on phone:** at 360 px wide, "fits." sits alone on the second line of the headline. Add `text-wrap: balance` to `.landing h1`, `h1.page` and `.lead-title`. styles.css:946, w360-landing (dark).

## Polish and bold changes

- **U37 Bold, the brief header:** Back, the title, the meta line, the sample banner, the progress card and the tabs take about 450 px before the first lead. On desktop, a finished brief can fold the progress card into a small chip beside Export. The chip would read "Ready · 7 s" and open the Steps. This is the shape the phone already uses. styles.css:1027-1045, w1280-brief-researcher.
- **U38 Bold, the browse list:** each award card is 230 to 290 px tall for a title, two facts and a source line. With 360 awards, that is a very long page. A denser row would help. The title and tags go on the left. Amount and deadline go in a fixed right column. "Open at source" becomes an icon link in the head. browse.js:75-104, styles.css:1406, w1280-awards.
- **U39 Bold, the signed-in landing:** a signed-in user sees the same marketing hero as a visitor. Show "Continue" with their latest brief and its kept count, above the two start buttons. w1280-after-welcome.
- **U40 Bold, cover points as two columns:** the points alternate "The record asks" and "You have" lines, with the chips in between. Lay each pair out as two columns. The record's ask goes left, the student's evidence goes right, and each column has its chip at the end. cover.js pointsBlock, w1280-cover-result.
- **U41 Bold, My Perch as a home page:** My Perch shows one list and a lot of empty space. It could hold three cards: Briefs, Saved awards and Your documents. Each card gets its own empty-state action. account.js:237-277, w1280-my-perch.
- **U42 Cut the student list preamble:** before the first student card there is a count heading, a grey paragraph and a grey "Unless a card says otherwise" box. That is about 180 px. Keep the count. Put the other two behind one "About this list" disclosure. w1280-brief-student.
- **U43 Say "Model text" once:** every grant card repeats the tag and "Checked against the records it cites." Say it once in the list header, and keep only the small tag on each card. app.js:1826-1829, w1280-brief-researcher.
- **U44 Owl in empty and error states:** the empty-list and could-not-load states are plain text. The Scout guest state uses the owl and reads warmer. Use the small owl in both. browse.js:184-193, w1280-awards-empty, w1280-researcher-list-error.
- **U45 Hide empty Compare columns:** when every row says "Not in the record", the column carries no information. Activity code on NSF rows is an example. Hide the column and add a note: "Activity code: none of these records state one". compare.js:100-125, w1280-brief-researcher-compare.
- **U46 Old award date too prominent:** "Award horizon" puts "30 Jun 2016 already passed" in large serif type. It is the boldest number on the profile page, but it is old news. When the date has passed, use the normal text size. app.js:1243-1255, w1280-brief-researcher-profile.
- **U47 Thread headings are raw keyword lists:** "lammps, molecular, dynamics, efficient" is shown as a bold heading. Show the words as small keyword chips under a plain "Thread 1" label. w1280-brief-researcher-profile.
- **U48 Key papers listed twice:** the Key papers rail repeats papers that are already listed under the threads. Mark the shared papers, or make the rail a compact list of links. w1280-brief-researcher-profile.
- **U49 Inconsistent capitals in profile values:** values that code writes, such as "bachelor's" and "full-time", sit next to "Year 3" and "May 2028". Capitalise the first letter in code. These are rule labels, not copied record text. w1280-brief-student-profile.
- **U50 Lowercase titles from the source pages:** some Temple titles are lowercase, such as "academic scholarships". These are copied record text, so the UI must not change them. Note them as a data-quality item for the Temple page parser. w1280-awards.
- **U51 Scout column narrower than the page:** the Scout column is 880 px wide inside a 1212 px page. Its left edge does not line up with the tabs above it. Align it to the tab row's left edge, or centre the tabs too. styles.css:1149, w1280-scout-clean.
- **U52 Stray "Local model" caption:** the centred caption under the answer floats between the answer and the question box. Move it into the answer's provenance line. scout.js:117, styles.css:1170, w1280-scout-clean.
- **U53 Consolidate the CSS layers:** `.cite` is defined four times. `.tag.sample` and `.foot p` are each defined twice, and the later rules override the earlier ones. Merge each into one rule before the S49 fixes, so the fixes do not fight hidden rules. styles.css:135, 882, 1116, 1127, 1221, 1455, 443, 967.
- **U54 Dark-mode sample banner too heavy:** in dark mode, the sample banner is a large dark-brown block. It is heavier than the content it labels. Use the dashed ember border on the surface colour, as the sample cards already do. styles.css:1129, w360-brief-student-compare (dark).
- **U55 Unequal gaps in the brief header:** in the brief header, Back sits 41 px under the banner, but the title sits 40 px under Back. Meanwhile the progress card sits 26 px under the meta line. Use one vertical rhythm, 8, 16 or 24 px, throughout the header. styles.css:982, w1280-brief-researcher.
