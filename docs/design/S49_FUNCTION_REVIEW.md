# S49 back-end function and feature review

Written 2026-11-04 by a fresh-context reviewer on branch `rebuild/v1`. Read-only review: no code changed, no paid call, no GPU job. Every count below comes from the files named or from read-only queries on the v1 test-deploy Postgres store on 2026-11-04. Each item has an ID, the place in the code, a way to measure it, and an effort guess.

How the counts were taken:

- **Store counts:** read-only SQL on the `records` table of the perch-v1 Postgres.
- **Scout removals:** the 59 drafts in `bench/results/scout_open_s45g/drafts.jsonl` were re-parsed with `perch/scout/answer.py` and each of the 206 removed sentences was sorted into a category by a small script. No model was called.

## Must-fix

- **M1 Open-anytime grants never reach a brief.** `perch/eligibility.py:123` rejects any posted record with no close date. 46 posted Grants.gov records have none, and many of them say "Proposals accepted anytime" or "accepted on a continuing basis" in `close_date_note`. Examples are NSF Plant Genome Research, Long Term Research in Environmental Biology and Research Infrastructure in the Social and Behavioral Sciences. Fix: keep a posted record whose note says "Proposals accepted anytime" or a close variant, and show the record's own words as the deadline. Do not keep stale continuing-basis notices, such as the EDA FY 2021 to FY 2023 ones: require a recent posted date as well. Measure: a hand list of which of the 46 pass `check`, and a test on one fixture record. The back-test is not affected because it sets `check_dates` to False. Effort: 1 hour.
- **M2 The level rule never fires on Temple-page awards.** `perch/student.py:199` reads only `level_of_study`. That field is empty on all 284 `temple_pages` scholarships, while `audience_level` is filled on 270 of them. So a PhD student gets undergraduate-only awards as "may be eligible", and an undergraduate gets graduate-only ones. Fix: when `level_of_study` is empty, map `audience_level` into the rule. Map "undergraduate" to bachelor's and "graduate" to graduate. Treat "mixed" as no rule, and leave "faculty" out of the student pool. Measure: on the SYNTHETIC persona-phd, count leads tagged undergraduate that show as "may be eligible", before and after. Do the same for persona-ug with graduate-tagged leads. Effort: 2 hours.
- **M3 Deadline words on the page are shown as "Not stated".** 43 Temple scholarships have a `deadline_quote` but no date, because the page gave no year. Scout's `facts` at `perch/scout/read_tools.py:102` returns "not stated in the record" for them. The student card does the same at `web/student.js:294`. Worse, `perch/scout/answer.py:410` then confirms "the record gives no deadline" as true, so a wrong claim passes the code check. Fix: return the quote as "the page's words" and make `_absent_fact` say False when a quote exists. Measure: the count of records where `facts` says not stated but a quote exists should go from 43 to 0. Add one unit test for the code check. Effort: 1 to 2 hours.
- **M4 A number inside an allowed link fails the check.** In `perch/validate.py:142` the number check runs over the whole sentence, link included. The link itself passes, because `source_url` is in `seen`. But the record id inside the link, such as 343980 in a grants.gov detail URL, is not in the evidence numbers, so the sentence is removed. This cost 2 removals in s45g. It will hit any skill that quotes a source link. Fix: remove links that passed the link check before counting numbers. Measure: one unit test, then a replay of s45g. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **M5 Fit text is cached for one day only.** The fit key at `perch/service.py:540` and the summary key at `perch/service.py:503` include the profile hash. That hash includes the as-of date at `perch/cache.py:62`. So the same papers and the same record get a new paid explanation every day. That costs about $0.08 and about 35 seconds per brief. The text does not depend on the date: the work ids and threads are already in the hash. Fix: build these two keys from a hash without the as-of date. Keep the date in the precache key. Measure: build one brief on two as-of dates and read `done_cached` in the stage line. The second day should show 10 of 10 cached and no new spend-ledger rows. Effort: 1 hour.
- **M6 The federal snapshot is a week old before Demo Day.** Grants.gov records were fetched 28 to 30 Oct. 56 posted records are now past their close date, and the browse list still shows them. Fix: refresh Grants.gov and NSF and bump the ingest epoch on 5 Nov. Then show "Records fetched on" with the oldest `fetched_at` of the list, copied from the records. Measure: the count of posted records past their close date after the refresh, and the newest `fetched_at`. Effort: 1 hour plus fetch time.
- **M7 Last year's date makes a student "not eligible".** 22 Temple records carry a deadline from an earlier cycle, from 2017 to Nov 2025. `perch/student.py:197` turns each one into "The deadline has passed" and rules the award out. Most of these awards recur every year. Recommendation for Dakshitha: when the page's date is more than about a month old, give "may be eligible" with the line "The page shows a past date, X. Check the page for this year's date." This changes a verdict, so it is a decision, not a routine fix. Measure: the verdicts of those 22 records for persona-ug. Effort: 1 hour.
- **M8 Staff awards sit in the student pool.** The scholarship kind holds advising awards such as "Professional Advisor", "Faculty Advisor", "New Advisor" and "Outstanding Advising Program Award". They are tagged undergraduate. Four more records are tagged faculty. `perch/service.py:645` sends all of them to the student brief. Fix: leave out `audience_level` faculty, and add a short reviewed exclusion list in `data/overrides/`. Measure: hand-list count in the student pool, from at least 8 down to 0. Effort: 1 hour.
- **M9 Temple internal faculty funding is unreachable from a researcher brief.** The 26 `temple_pages` opportunities have empty `eligible_applicants`, and most have no close date. Examples are Blue Sky Initiative, I-SPARK, Pivot Bridge Fund and STEM Research Grants. `perch/eligibility.py` drops every one of them with "no applicant types in the record". Browse with the Temple-only filter does list them, because `temple_pages` is in `TEMPLE_SOURCES` at `perch/store/denorm.py:16` and browse has no eligibility filter. But the brief never ranks them. Fix: give Temple-page opportunities their own rule set, with a Temple applicant assumed and past-cycle dates handled as in M7. Then show them as a separate "Temple internal" group, never mixed with federal leads. This changes what a brief shows, so it is a decision for Dakshitha. Measure: the count of those 26 records that pass, and one demo researcher's brief. Effort: 2 to 3 hours.

## Q49 Scout tuning plan

Baseline, s45g: 10 of 33 in-scope answers passed on the first or second draft, and 23 were shown flagged with sentences removed. Across all 59 drafts the rules removed 206 sentences. Of the 128 removed for no citation, up to 75 come straight after a sentence that does cite a record. The categories are a heuristic sort, and the drafts carry no tool log, so the replay in Q0 is approximate until tool calls are recorded. Target for this round: at least 20 of 33 pass, with every sentence in the guard set still removed.

- **Q0 Build an offline replay first.** `bench/scout_open.py` writes `drafts.jsonl` but has no replay mode, and the drafts do not store the tool calls. Add `--replay DIR`. It re-grades every stored draft with the current `answer.py` and prints removals by reason, Layer 1 passes and flagged answers. To rebuild `seen` with no model call, take every id the drafts of a question cite, then resolve it in the store or in the SYNTHETIC persona. From now on, also write each turn's tool calls to the file so later replays are exact. Measure: a replay of s45g with today's rules gives 206 removals. Effort: 2 to 3 hours.
- **Q1 Add a guard set before loosening anything.** Write about 15 sentences that must always be removed, in `tests/`. Include an invented amount, an invented deadline, an uncited "you are eligible", a rank that is wrong, a fake link, a named award in General advice, and a sample cited with a public record. Every Q item below must leave all 15 removed. Measure: a test that passes. Effort: 1 hour.
- **Q2 Give whole-brief facts a record to cite.** This is the main structural cause. `read_brief` at `perch/scout/read_tools.py:274` returns `user:brief` only when the brief is empty, and `search_records` returns no id for the search itself. So sentences like "All six leads close on the same date" or "Your top two leads are both kept" cannot be cited at all. Fix: always add a code-written `user:brief` record that lists the count, and for each lead its rank, title, sponsor, deadline text and kept flag. Add one `search:N` record per search call that gives the query, the count and the titles. Layer 1 then finds the numbers, and `code_check` already checks rank and kept claims. Two traps for the builder. First, `PLACEHOLDER_IDS` at `perch/scout/answer.py:183` and the empty-list rule at line 524 key on the id `user:brief`, so that rule must key on whether the brief was empty instead. Second, the summary record lists public leads only, because one citation of a record naming both kinds would mix a sample with public records, against rule 11. Measure: on replay, 26 brief and CV facts plus 5 cites of `user:brief` plus part of the 25 "nothing found" variants. Effort: 3 hours.
- **Q3 Let a cited sentence carry its citation to the next one.** 75 of 128 no-citation removals come straight after a cited sentence, such as "There are two tracks." That 75 is an upper bound: it counts across lines and overlaps Q4 to Q6. Only 7 follow a cited sentence on the same line. Rule: an uncited sentence inherits the ids of the sentence before it on the same line, or on the line above when that line is prose and not a heading. It must then pass Layer 1 and MiniCheck against those records. It may not inherit when it holds a fit word or when the cited record is a sample. Change the prompt line at `perch/scout/texts.py:224` to match. Measure: replay removals and MiniCheck flags, with the guard set still removed. Effort: 2 hours.
- **Q4 Accept the fixed headings without a colon.** `is_heading` at `perch/scout/answer.py:488` needs a colon, bold or a hash mark. So a bare line "What the record asks for" falls through to no citation. This cost 20 removals. Fix: a one-sentence line that fully matches `STRUCTURAL_RE` is a heading whatever its punctuation. Measure: replay, expecting 20 fewer removals. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **Q5 Stop splitting bold numbered titles.** `split_sentences` at `perch/scout/answer.py:328` cuts "**1. SBE Postdoctoral...**" into "**1." and "SBE...**". It also refuses a bold record title longer than 12 words. This cost 9 removals. Fix: do not split after a list number that follows bold marks. Accept a bold line whose text matches the title of a record cited under it, at any length. Measure: replay, expecting 9 fewer. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **Q6 Accept the disclaimer with a short help tail.** `DISCLAIMER_RE` at `perch/scout/answer.py:534` needs the exact sentence. The model writes "Perch does not confirm personal eligibility, so check with the sponsor or a Temple office." This cost 4 removals. Fix: allow the disclaimer followed by a help tail that passes `_no_record_claims`. Measure: replay, expecting 4 fewer. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **Q7 Let a checked rank number pass Layer 1.** "Rank 1 is the SBE Postdoctoral Research Fellowship" fails on the number 1 before `code_check` can confirm the rank. s45g had 14 layer-1 number removals. M4 covers 2 of them. Most of the rest are rank numbers, and a few are counts such as "1, 2 and 3" inside a CV sentence. Fix: when the brief was read and `code_check` confirms the rank, drop that number from the Layer 1 count. Leave other small numbers to Q2's brief record. Measure: replay, and a guard case with a wrong rank. Effort: 1 hour.
- **Q8 Check "nothing found" claims about the brief by code.** 25 removals were short absence sentences such as "None of your leads has a deadline in its record." After Q2, require `[user:brief]` on these. Then let `_absent_fact` check every brief row, instead of asking for one fixed template. Measure: replay of the 25. Effort: 1 hour after Q2.
- **Q9 Widen the fixed section names a little.** Add "Differences", "Similarities", "Your top lead", "Who may apply, per the record" and "What the records say" to `STRUCTURAL_HEADINGS` at `perch/scout/texts.py:117`. Keep headings that state a result, such as "Excluded: ceiling under your limit", removed. Measure: replay of the 19 heading removals. About 8 should pass and the rest stay removed. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **Q10 Let an offer name a record returned this turn.** "Would you like me to read the Amgen Scholars record in full?" is removed as a named award by `perch/scout/answer.py:668`. An offer that names a record Scout just returned states nothing new. Fix: pass the returned titles as allowed names for offers and help lines only, not for General advice. Also allow a leading "Or" before an offer stem. Measure: replay of 2 named-award and 4 question removals. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **Q11 Allow a short tail after an empty-list sentence.** "You have no typed entries to add context [user:entries]." is removed at `perch/scout/answer.py:524` because something follows the template. This cost 6 removals. Fix: accept the template stem followed by a "to" or "that" tail with no number, no fit word and no award name. Measure: replay of the 6. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **Q12 Write process sentences by code, not by the model.** Six removals were the model narrating its own steps, such as "Each search returned up to 8 results, so there may be more." The "how Scout read it" line already shows the steps. Fix: code adds one fixed line per search, such as "I searched for X and found N records [search:1].", backed by Q2's search record. Tell the model not to describe its own steps. Measure: replay, and a count of process sentences in the next run. Effort: 1 to 2 hours.
- **Q13 Change the prompt, then run once.** In `perch/scout/texts.py:207`, ask for one fact per sentence with no "so ..." follow-on, and an id at the end of every bullet. Ask for every heading to end with a colon, `[user:brief]` for whole-brief facts, and no narration of steps. Measure: one rerun of the open bench, at about $1.10 under the $1.30 cap. Compare first-draft removals by reason with s45g. Effort: 1 hour plus the run.
- **Q14 Repair only the failing sentences.** Today a failed answer is written again in full, and 23 of 33 answers needed a second draft. That second draft roughly doubles the cost of those answers. Fix: send back only the failing sentences with their reasons, ask for replacements, and grade the merged answer. Measure: dollars per in-scope answer, about $0.032 in s45g, and the pass count. Effort: 3 hours.
- **Q15 Make the bench brief realistic.** `bench/scout_open.py:128` builds the shortlist from the records that close next. On the run date all six leads closed on 4 Nov 2026, so every deadline and ordering question was degenerate. Fix: use a stored ranked brief of a demo researcher with a pinned as-of date, plus the SYNTHETIC student persona. Measure: the deadline questions get distinct dates. Effort: 1 hour.

## Should-fix

- **S1 Scout's search is keyword and alphabetical.** `search_records` at `perch/scout/read_tools.py:235` calls `browse` with a substring match. Browse returns the first 25 by name, and the results are then sorted by word count. On the back-test, keyword-only retrieval found 0 of 48 targeted awards in the top 10. Fix: search the dense index used by briefs, and let the opportunity filters run through `eligibility.check`. Measure: hit@10 of `search_records` on the 48 back-test thread queries, against the keyword baseline. Effort: 3 hours.
- **S2 Scout search does not mark closed or forecast records.** `facts` gives a deadline text, but not whether the record is open now. Fix: add an `open` field written by code from the status and the dates, and have the prompt say when a record is closed. Measure: a unit test and one bench question. Effort: 1 hour.
- **S3 Scout cannot see Industry leads.** `read_brief` at `perch/scout/read_tools.py:282` drops route B. A question such as "which companies did you find" then gets an empty or wrong answer. Either add the Industry leads as their own list, labelled inferred, or have the gate answer that Scout covers grants only. Measure: one bench question. Effort: 1 hour.
- **S4 Retrieval wastes almost half its candidates.** `perch/brief.py` takes the top 200, then the eligibility filter keeps a median of 114, ranging from 97 to 117, in the 23 logged briefs. Fix: filter the dense search to posted, applicant-ok and open records first, so the reranker gets 100 that are all eligible. Measure: the `eligible` count in `brief_timings.jsonl`, and back-test hit@10 and MRR with the keyword baseline beside them. Effort: 2 hours.
- **S5 Near-copies of one call may fill the top 10.** NIH often posts one call as R01, R21 and clinical-trial variants. Check first: count distinct title stems in the top 10 of the five S38 cost briefs. If copies are common, show one card per family with its variants listed by code. Measure: distinct families in the top 10. Score back-test hits by family, so collapsing cannot hide a hit. Effort: 2 to 3 hours.
- **S6 Temple-page records are ranked on their name only.** `record_text` at `perch/student.py:353` embeds the name, purpose, fields and notes. All 360 Temple records have no purpose, and the notes average 137 characters. Fix: add the page owner, the quoted eligibility sentences from `field_quotes` and the page heading. Measure: mean embedded text length by source, and a new SYNTHETIC hand key of about 20 Temple records for persona-ug. Effort: 2 hours.
- **S7 The same award appears twice for students.** 9 name groups are duplicated across `temple_pages` and `temple_sdfa`, among them Gilman, Truman, NSF GRFP and Diamond Research Scholars. Both copies enter the student pool at `perch/service.py:645`. Fix: merge by normalised name, keep the record with more fields, and list the other page as "also listed on". Measure: 9 groups down to 0 in the pool, and no duplicate in a student top 10. Effort: 1 to 2 hours.
- **S8 Two words for one level.** `audience_level` holds both "undergraduate" with 125 records and "bachelor's" with 44 SDFA records, at `perch/store/denorm.py:34`. So the browse level filter misses one group. Fix: normalise to one word when columns are built, and re-run the denorm. Measure: facet counts. Effort: 1 hour.
- **S9 Browse is not open-first.** `perch/store/postgres.py:154` orders by name only. The `closes_after` filter at line 138 also drops every undated record, which is 287 of 310 Temple records. Fix: order by open dated records soonest first, then undated, then past dates last. Keep undated records under the open filter with a "no date on the page" label. Measure: the first page of `/awards?temple_only=true`. Effort: 1 to 2 hours.
- **S10 A student brief lists every ruled-out record.** `choose` at `perch/student.py:417` appends every "not eligible" record among the 100 retrieved. Fix: cap these at 5, and leave out records ruled out only by M7's old-cycle dates. Measure: leads per student brief for the three SYNTHETIC personas. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **S11 The student brief ignores the student's school.** 153 records are scoped to one school, but the student brief has no school or Temple-only option. Browse has both. Fix: take school and Temple-only as brief options. Rank another school's records after the student's own, with a code line naming the school that lists them. Measure: other-school records in the top 10 for a SYNTHETIC persona with a school set. Effort: 3 hours.
- **S12 Budgets a user hits without warning.** The defaults at `perch/redisx.py:215` to `222` are $0.50 a day per account, $0.10 per guest and $3.00 a day for all guests. A researcher brief costs $0.077 to $0.096 and an in-scope Scout answer about $0.032. So an account gets about 6 briefs or about 15 Scout answers a day. A guest gets one brief, sometimes cut short, and all guests together get about 36 fresh briefs a day. Fix: return the remaining budget with every job and show it before a brief starts. Refuse up front when the estimate is above what is left, instead of stopping midway. Measure: a test with the fake provider. Effort: 2 hours.
- **S13 The nightly pre-cache saves the wrong part.** `compute` at `perch/precache.py:97` stores the ranking only. On the GPU profile that part takes 1.4 to 1.8 seconds, and the model wait takes about 35 seconds. Fix: after M5, pre-generate the profile summary and the top 10 fit texts for Temple-affiliated researchers. This is public input. It costs about $0.08 per researcher. Measure: first-brief wall time against the 37.7 second median, and the spend ledger. Effort: 2 hours.
- **S14 Rerun grounding on the v1 model.** The pass rates on 30 leads date from S8, before many changes, and the cloud model there passed 24 of 30. Fix: rerun the 30-lead grounding bench with Sonnet 5.5 fit text and MiniCheck on GPU. Measure: passed both checks out of 30, at about $0.30. Effort: 1 hour.
- **S15 The model's fit number is generated and thrown away.** `perch/skills/fit.py:36` asks for a 0 to 100 fit and a reasoning field. Neither is shown. The reasoning is most of the 7,076 output tokens per brief. Fix: either drop the number and cap the reasoning length, or use the number only to flag leads where the model calls the link weak. Measure: output tokens and dollars per brief, and the grounding pass count from S14. Effort: 1 hour.

## Features to add

- **F1 Parent announcements from award history.** Parent announcements are almost never found: 2 of 78 in the top 10. A researcher's NIH awards from RePORTER give the activity codes and institutes they already win. Code can pick the matching open parent announcements and show them in their own group, "Open to any topic". The award records come from `perch/awards.py`, and the group is added in `perch/brief.py`. Measure: back-test parent hit@10 with the date cut-off and the keyword baseline, against 0.026. Effort: 3 hours.
- **F2 Say what was hidden and why.** `RouteABrief.rejected` already holds the reasons. Show one code-written line, such as "12 calls hidden: 5 closed, 4 for small businesses, 3 career-stage". Let the user open that list. Measure: shown on every brief, with the counts matching the stored `rejected`. Effort: 2 hours.
- **F3 Deadline file for a calendar.** Offer a download of kept leads' close dates as an `.ics` file, with dates copied from the records and nothing sent. Build it next to the exports in `perch/export.py`. Measure: a test comparing the file's dates with the records. Effort: 2 hours.
- **F4 Keep from a Scout result, and edit a filter by code.** SCOUT_SPEC section 6 already proposes both. Each search result gets a Keep button. Removing a chip reruns that search with no model call. Measure: Keep from Scout in the UI walk, and zero model calls on a chip edit. Effort: 3 hours.
- **F5 A "this year's date" watch for students.** For awards with only a past or yearless date, the nightly ingest re-reads the page. It shows "New date on the page" when a quoted date for this cycle appears. The re-read belongs in `perch/temple_dataset.py`. Measure: the count of records that gain a current date after one run. Effort: 3 hours.
- **F6 Why-not for students in one line.** For each "may be eligible", code already knows the first unknown. Put that first unknown on the card face, such as "Your CV does not state a GPA". Then the student sees the one thing to add. The line is `Verdict.lines` from `perch/student.py`, so only the card changes. Measure: the line is shown on every "may be eligible" card. Effort: 1 hour.
- **F7 Show the source refresh date.** Next to every list, show "Federal records fetched D. Temple pages read D.", copied from `fetched_at`. Add it to the list response in `perch/browse/routes.py:49`. Measure: the dates match the store. Effort: 1 hour.
- **F8 A Scout answer card for deadlines across kept leads.** The Deadlines button already exists at `perch/scout/buttons.py:138`. Add an "Add to calendar" link from it once F3 lands. Measure: covered by F3's test. Effort: 30 minutes.
