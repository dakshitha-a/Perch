## Demo script

Five minutes, presenter on a laptop with the projector, audience on phones. Before it starts: D7's morning checks are done, D8's port test passed, and a QR code for the public URL is on the first slide.

1. **0:00 Landing, in a private window:** a signed-in window opens on My Perch, so keep two windows. The private window is signed out and shows what the audience sees. Show the tagline and the three promises in the footer. Say: "Scan the code, tap Student, then Try a sample student." Expected: the guest sample, once D4 is fixed. Fallback: say "Try it as a guest", the small link under the buttons.
2. **0:30 Signed-in window, My Perch, then the researcher brief:** open the pre-built researcher brief with one click. Expected: "Ready", the Grants tab, Match pills, D3's centre line on any P30 or P50. Fallback: open `example.html`.
3. **1:00 One card, opened:** open "Why it fits" on the top individual-lab lead, then press Evidence. Point out that the date and the amount in the panel are copied from the record. Expected: cited text and the evidence panel on the right. Fallback: the same card in the example.
4. **1:40 Temple internal and Industry:** one tap each. Check the Temple tab in the morning, and skip it if a closed call shows, as in D18. On an industry card, read the line "Inferred from public signals; not verified." Expected: two short lists. Fallback: skip; say the example has them.
5. **2:10 Ask Scout:** show the answer asked in the morning, then press the Deadlines chip live, which code answers at once. Do not type a new open question on stage. Expected: cited dates in seconds. Fallback: `example.html?tab=scout`.
6. **2:50 Compare and hand-off:** open Compare with the two kept leads. Scroll to the hand-off drafted in the morning. Point to the "Model text" label, the ERA line, and the words "Perch never sends it". Do not press "Open in my mail app" on the projector. Fallback: `example.html?tab=compare`.
7. **3:40 Switch to Student:** open the pre-built sample-student brief. Show the "Sample data" badge, a card with ✓ and ? rule lines, and on the Samples tab the card that fails on GPA, 3.50 asked and 3.40 on the CV. Find where that card sits in the morning, since not-eligible cards may be folded. Show cover points only if the morning run of one sample card came back with no flags. Fallback: `example.html?side=student`.
8. **4:30 Close:** "Every claim cites a public record. Nothing is sent. The audience is using it now." Put the QR code back up.

- **If sign-in fails:** do steps 2 to 7 on the example pages. They need no sign-in and call no model.
- **If the deploy is down:** run the fallback build, tag `v0.1-demo-fallback`, rehearsed once in D7.
- **If guests report empty reasons:** that is D16, the guest budget. The lists, dates and rules still work. Say so and move on.
- **Slow steps to avoid live:** a fresh researcher brief, about 38 to 50 seconds; cover points, up to two minutes; an open Scout question; Run again.
