# QA cycle 5 (round 5, the last of the final QA cycles)

## How it was done

This was a black-box acceptance test on 1 Nov 2026, from 06:48 to 08:05, of the build on `main` at ca0c7fb. All the work ran on the dev server (port 8091), restarted on that commit at 06:48 with `build/devdemo/serve.sh`. The demo server (port 8090) was not tested. Cloud was never turned on: its dialog was opened and cancelled, and no script posts to the cloud switch. Three testers worked in parallel: one on Scout by API, one on exports, hand-offs, notes, Help and privacy, and one in the browser on the demo script and every screen. Answer times below are under that load unless marked quiet.

**Machine checks:**
- `make test` ran twice. Each run passed 377 tests.
- The full walk (`scripts/ux_walk.py`, axe 4.10.2) covered 214 states. It found 0 errors, 0 axe violations and 0 sideways scroll. 84 targets under 24 px are the hidden file inputs and inline text links, as in round 4.
- `make package` built `perch-0.1.0-ca0c7fb.zip` (27.6 MB, 431 files). It opens and tests clean. Its listing holds no review shots, dev store, `.env` or local notes, and a scan of the extracted tree found no local path, email, tunnel host or session link.

**Demo rehearsal (`scripts/demo_rehearsal.py`, three fresh sessions per flow):**
- `--kept top2` (the demo): 15 of 15 (researcher 9 of 9, student 3 of 3, persona-1 documents 3 of 3).
- `--kept none`: 15 of 15.
- `--kept weak2`: 15 of 15.
- One more `top2` run on a quiet server: questions 1 to 3 took 5, 5 and 6 seconds.
- The three demo answers were word for word the same in the three `top2` sessions, the laptop run and the phone run. The model runs at temperature 0, so repeated runs are close to one sample; the kept-lead variants are the real spread.
- The rehearsal asks only the scripted questions. G2 to G4 and G12 are off-script questions it does not ask.

**Truncation:** 1,706 calls in the demo Ollama journal since 06:48. Calls with "truncated" above 0: 0. The largest call was 6,773 of 8,192 tokens.

**Browser and settings:** Playwright Chromium. Phone 390 × 844 and laptop 1280 × 860, light and dark. 200% (640 × 430 at scale 2), 400% (320 × 215 at scale 4) and 320 × 640 reflow. Keyboard-only through the researcher flow. Back, Forward, reload and End session on every tab. Two briefs built at once. 83 screenshots are in `build/review/qa5/`; each one cited below was opened and looked at. The text of every screen was dumped and read beside its screenshot.

**Audiences:** PI-4 (A5026494099), the demo researcher; PI-9 (A5108616139), a live build by id, text only; the SYNTHETIC students persona-ug, persona-masters and persona-phd; and the SYNTHETIC researcher persona-1 on the documents-only path.

**Scout:** 51 API conversations, 255 questions, each in a fresh session, including twelve-turn conversations for PI-4 and persona-phd typed as professors and students type, and the budget stop. Plus the demo questions in the browser twice and the example questions once. Transcripts, aliased, are in `build/review/qa5/scout/` (turns numbered from 1). Under load the median answer took 17 s, the slowest 50 s. A wrong-record check ran over every answer: 703 citation checks on 617 lines in 237 answers; 32 lines were flagged and read; 1 line, the same in 3 runs, cites the wrong record (G7).

**Content checks:** every export (Markdown, print page, JSON) for PI-4, the three students and persona-1, students and persona-1 masked and unmasked; every hand-off and note; every mail link decoded; about 145 Help claims checked against code or a live response. Aliased copies are in `build/review/qa5/exports/`.

**Counts:** 5 must-fix, 12 should-fix, 6 could-fix. Of 107 earlier items, 99 pass, 6 fail (T6, T10, T17, F3, F15, F16, all Scout questions off the demo script; each has a must-fix row: G2, G3, G4, G12), 1 was dropped in round 3 (T19) and 1 is left for S32 (F28).
