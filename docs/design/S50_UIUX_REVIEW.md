## Must-fix

- **V1 Heading focus box, leftover:** after each route change, the page heading shows a full-width blue box. It shows on awards, funding, samples, welcome and the student start, in both themes. The cause is the global `:focus-visible` rule hitting headings that code focuses. A scoped rule such as `h1[tabindex="-1"]:focus { outline: none; }` fixes it and keeps the screen-reader move. Code: web/styles.css:94, web/account.js:62, web/browse.js:335, web/browse.js:402, web/student.js:140. Screenshots: w1280-awards, w1280-welcome, w1280-s49b-welcome, w360-awards, dark w1280-samples.
- **V2 Card titles squeezed, made worse in round 1:** the card head is a two-column grid. The right column holds the match pill, the eligibility pill and two buttons, and it never wraps. So the title column is about 450 px wide on researcher cards and about 310 px on student cards, in a card that is 840 px wide. Short titles such as "SYNTHETIC Harbor Prize" break over two lines. Move Keep and Dismiss to a footer row of the card, or put the pills under the title. Code: web/styles.css:1506, web/styles.css:1508, web/app.js:1842, web/app.js:1961, web/student.js:387. Screenshots: w1280-s49-student-list, w1280-brief-student, w1280-brief-researcher.
- **V3 "Score" sort is out of order on Temple internal:** the button says Score, but the code sorts by the server's rank. On route T the Match numbers read 6, 55, 2, 45, 44 from top to bottom. Those two cards are planted SYNTHETIC records, and the Grants route is in order, so real data may hide this. The label still promises an order the code does not give. Sort by `match_score` when the label says Score, or rename the button "Perch's order", as the phone compare already does. Code: web/app.js:1529-1535, web/app.js:1557. Screenshot: w1280-s49a-route-t.
- **V4 The same "To check" line on every student card, leftover P6:** all ten cards on the sample brief say "The listing says who may apply; the award's own page sets the full rules." It tells the student nothing and makes the cards look copied. Show a code-written line that says what the rules checked. If that is not ready, show the sentence once above the list. Code: web/student.js:393. Screenshot: w1280-brief-student.
- **V5 Hints repeated on every card, made worse in round 1:** two sentences now repeat on every card of one list. One is "A model writes these from your CV and entries. You put them in your own words." under each Cover-letter points button. The other is "Written reason withheld: it failed the citation check." under a notice that already says so. Show each one once above the list, as the guest line already does. Code: web/cover.js:10-11, web/cover.js:179, web/app.js:1687, web/app.js:1924, web/student.js:399. Screenshots: w1280-s49-student-list, w1280-s49-student-list-clean, dark w1280-brief-researcher.
- **V6 Fact columns jump between cards, made worse in round 1:** Closes and Amount sit in a wrapping flex row. So the Amount column starts at three different places in one list: 106 px, 223 px and 239 px from the card's inner edge. Use a grid with two fixed columns, about 220 px each, so the facts line up down the list. Code: web/styles.css:1515. Screenshots: w1280-brief-researcher, w1280-s49a-route-t, w1280-s49-student-list.
- **V7 Search button doubled border, leftover:** the Search button sits inside the bordered search box. That gives two borders side by side. On a phone it becomes a full-width button inside the box. Put the button outside the box, or drop the outer border and give the input its own border. The clear "×" inside the box is also the browser's own blue; style it with the ink colour. Code: web/styles.css:957-961, web/styles.css:1179-1180, web/browse.js:313-316. Screenshots: w1280-awards, w1280-awards-empty, w360-awards, w360-s49b-funding.
- **V8 Form column off-centre, made worse in round 1:** the form screen is 760 px wide, but its cards and buttons are capped at 680 px and sit on the left. On welcome and account the cards sit 40 px left of centre, with 80 px of empty column on their right. On the student start the cards are 760 px and the "Find awards that fit" button is 680 px, so the two right edges do not line up. Use one width for the whole column. Code: web/pages.css:103, web/styles.css:1425, web/styles.css:1428. Screenshots: w1280-welcome, w1280-account, w1280-student-start.
- **V9 Phone lists start two screens down:** on a phone, the awards list shows 17 school chips in about ten rows. The funding list stacks search, two selects, two switches and a hint. The first record appears after 700 to 1100 px of controls. Make the chip row one line that scrolls sideways on a phone. Fold level, sort and the switches into one "Filters" button. - **V10 Field and button borders too faint:** the border token is 1.22:1 against the page in light mode and 1.29:1 against a card in dark mode. WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1 when the border is what shows a field or button. The search box and the secondary buttons rely on that border, and in dark mode "Check my fit with my CV" almost disappears. Add a stronger border token for inputs and secondary buttons, and keep the faint one for card edges. Code: web/styles.css:24, web/styles.css:53, web/styles.css:68. Screenshots: dark w1280-landing, w1280-awards, dark w1280-s49b-awards-signed.
