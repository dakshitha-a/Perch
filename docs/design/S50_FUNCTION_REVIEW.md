## Must-fix

- **G1 Graduate and postdoc awards pass for undergraduates.** `AUDIENCE_LEVELS` at `perch/student.py:194` has no entry for "mixed", so no level rule fires on 75 Temple-page awards. For persona-ug, 8 awards with "Graduate" in the name come out "may be eligible". Examples are "Graduate Student Research Grants", "Fox Graduate Program Merit-Based Scholarships" and "The Marie Rittenhouse Masters Scholarship". The research office page files NRSA F32 and F33, which are postdoc fellowships, as "mixed" student scholarships. It files NRSA F31, the PhD fellowship, as "faculty", so `student_pool` at `perch/student.py:436` hides it from PhD students. Fix: when the tag is "mixed" or empty, take the level from a word in the award's own name. "Graduate", "Doctoral", "Dissertation" and "Master's" mean graduate. "Undergraduate" means bachelor's. Leave out names with "Postdoctoral" or F32 or F33, and treat F31 as graduate. Measure: rerun the verdict script. Wrong-level names for persona-ug should go from 10 to 0, and persona-phd should see F31. Effort: 1.5 hours.
- **G2 One award shows twice, with opposite verdicts.** `student_pool` at `perch/student.py:436-447` merges copies only across the two sources, and only on the exact normalised name. A looser name match finds 16 groups covering 34 records. Examples: three copies of LAURA, four of Tuition Exchange, three of Diamond Research Scholars and three of the Fulbright U.S. Student Program. The worst case is a UK graduate scholarship. The fellowships office lists "a UK graduate scholarship" for seniors, with class year 4. The Graduate School page lists "a UK graduate scholarships" tagged graduate. So a senior gets "may be eligible" on one card and "not eligible" on the other. Fix: use a looser key that ignores a plural "s", "Program", "Scholarship(s)" and anything in brackets. Merge copies within one source too. When the fellowships office lists the award, keep its level and class year. Keep a short reviewed list in `data/overrides/` for names that look alike but are different awards. Measure: 16 groups down to only the reviewed exceptions, and no award twice in a persona's top 10. Effort: 2 hours.
- **G3 Notices that fund nothing reach the brief.** `check` at `perch/eligibility.py:145` has no rule for a notice of intent or a request for information. Three such notices pass today, among them DE-FOA-0003648 and the PROSPECT notice DE-FOA-0003662. In s49b the bench's top lead was the request for information DE-FOA-0003671. Scout had to explain that it is "not a funding opportunity" (answers i11, i15 and i17). Fix: add a title rule next to `TITLE_RESTRICTIONS` at `perch/eligibility.py:54` for "Request for Information", "RFI", "Notice of Intent", "NOI" and "Sources Sought". Its hidden reason reads "a notice, not a funding call", with a `HIDDEN_GROUPS` row. Measure: eligible notices 3 to 0, and the hidden count on one brief. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **G4 Refresh the federal records today, not in S52.** Grants.gov records were last fetched on 30 Oct. 56 posted records are already past their close date. 65 eligible calls close within 14 days of 6 Nov. Demo Day is 6 Nov, so the refresh that HANDOFF puts in S52 must run on 5 Nov. Then run `scripts/redenorm.py`. Measure: the newest `fetched_at` should be 5 Nov, and the count of posted records past their close date should be about 0. Effort: 1 hour plus fetch time.
- **G5 The Temple internal group shows stale and wrong pages.** `check_temple_internal` at `perch/eligibility.py:212-231` keeps a page date of any age with the past-cycle line. 6 of the 26 pages carry dates from 2021 to 2024. "Department Faculty Research Award" would show 25 Mar 2021 on a demo card. The pages for K01, K02, K07 and K08 describe NIH mechanisms, not Temple money. "Undergraduate Honors Senior Project Grants" is tagged faculty, and its quote says the deadline "has passed". "Graduate Student Fellowships" is tagged mixed and reaches researchers. "Innovation Flight Fund" appears twice. Fix: give past-cycle dates a maximum age of about 13 months, and hide older pages with "page last dated X". Leave out names with an NIH code or with student words, and dedupe by name. The maximum age changes what a brief shows, so record it in DECISIONS.md. Measure: hand-list the pages that pass, now 24 or more, and check one demo researcher's Temple group. Effort: 1 hour.
- **G6 P6 is still open: "may be eligible" says nothing.** `check` at `perch/student.py:293` gives the same fixed line to 231 of persona-ug's 241 "may be eligible" verdicts. Fix: code writes which rules ran, such as "Checked: level, undergraduate; year 3 of 1 to 3." It then quotes the listing's own sentence from `eligibility.notes`, such as "For first-year undergraduates, sophomores, or juniors." This is F6 from round 1. Measure: may-be cards with only the fixed line go from 231 to 0. Effort: 1.5 hours, plus the card change.
- **G7 Student cards show a wall of "Not stated".** None of the 76 fellowships-office records has a deadline, an amount, a deadline quote or a `details_url`. Of the 284 Temple-page awards, 15 have a date, 58 have deadline words and 51 have an amount. So a student's top 10 reads "Not stated" twice on almost every card; the fixture screenshot shows 8 of 8. Fix: for a fellowships-office record, the facts read "See the award's page", and the page link sits next to them. This is honest, because that list never gives these facts. The change goes in `web/student.js:373-378` (`MISSING`). Measure: walk screenshot. Effort: 30 minutes.
- **G8 The Funding list shows "5 Oct 2076" for open-anytime NSF programmes.** `deadlineText` at `web/browse.js:64` uses the record's words only when standard dates apply. The far-date rule at `web/app.js:1916` and `placeholder_close` at `perch/datefmt.py:67` are not used there. This hits 31 posted NSF programmes dated 2076, among them BIO Core and MPS Chemistry. A further 12 posted records carry 1 Jan 2099. Four eligible records have dates from 2031 to 2044 and no note, among them FA238424S2233, which closes 22 Aug 2044. Fix: browse uses the card's far-date rule. A far date with no note reads "No close date in practice; see the record". Measure: the first page of Funding sorted by name. Effort: 30 minutes.
