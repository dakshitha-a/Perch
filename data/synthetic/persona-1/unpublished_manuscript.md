Title: "Weeks, not minutes: home insole recordings reveal early gait instability"

Abstract (draft). We recorded plantar force with instrumented insoles in a SYNTHETIC
cohort of older adults during daily life for up to eight weeks. A graph
representation of each gait cycle, with sensors as nodes and force transfer as
edges, captured variability that short laboratory walks missed. Participants with
probable REM sleep behaviour disorder showed higher cycle-to-cycle variability than
controls. These invented results motivate a longitudinal study of home gait as an
early marker of Parkinson's disease. Authors: R. Vale and SYNTHETIC co-authors.
