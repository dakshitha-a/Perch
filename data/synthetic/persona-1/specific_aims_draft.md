Working title: "Home insole sensing to detect prodromal Parkinson's gait change"

Parkinson's disease is usually diagnosed after substantial neuron loss, when motor
signs are already clear. Subtle changes in how force moves through the foot during
walking may appear years earlier, but clinic visits capture only a few strides.
Our preliminary SYNTHETIC data suggest that stride-to-stride force variability,
measured at home over weeks, separates people with REM sleep behaviour disorder
from age-matched controls. [Draft note: confirm the effect size before submission.]

**Aim 1.** Build and validate a low-cost insole system that records ground reaction
force at home for 8 weeks, with on-device processing so raw data stay with the
participant.

**Aim 2.** Test whether a graph model of the gait cycle distinguishes prodromal
participants from controls, and whether its output tracks change over 12 months.

**Aim 3.** Establish a shared, de-identified reference dataset and analysis code for
other groups.
